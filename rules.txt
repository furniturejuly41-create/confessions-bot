📜 Bot Rules & Regulations

To keep the community safe, respectful, and meaningful, please follow these guidelines when using the bot:

1. Stay Relevant: This space is mainly for sharing confessions, experiences, and thoughts.
 - Avoid using it just to ask random questions you could easily Google or ask in the right place.
 - Some student-related questions may be approved if they benefit the community.

2. Respectful Communication: Sensitive topics (political, religious, cultural, etc.) are allowed but must be discussed with respect.

3. No Harmful Content: You may mention names, but at your own risk.
 - The bot and admins are not responsible for any consequences.
 - If someone mentioned requests removal, their name will be taken down.

4. Names & Responsibility: Do not share personal identifying information about yourself or others.

5. Anonymity & Privacy: don't reveal private details of others (contacts, adress, etc.) without consent.

6. Constructive Environment: Keep confessions genuine. Avoid spam, trolling, or repeated submissions.
 - Respect moderators' decisions on approvals, edits, or removals.

Use this space to connect, share, and learn, not to spread misinformation or cause unnecessary drama.
